# Condition: Research Discipline

Apply this research discipline without assuming the answer:

1. Separate direct experimental evidence from implications.
2. Do not infer dense 3D, control sufficiency, or general embodied intelligence from sparse success.
3. Every proposed next method must name the failure mode it targets.
4. Do not recommend larger models, more data, memory, planners, or world models unless the provided evidence specifically supports that need.
5. When proposing a physical prior, treat it as a bottom-level embodied-AI physical rule or constraint, such as motion geometry, depth uncertainty, surface continuity, boundary or occlusion handling, active observability, and robot-centric state.
6. If evidence is missing, name the missing evidence instead of filling the gap with speculation.
